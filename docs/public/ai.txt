description: A local touchscreen controller for a Mac. EspDesktop pairs its native macOS menu-bar app with the ESP32-S3 4848S040 to launch approved apps, open folders and websites, run shortcuts, arrange windows, control media and volume, and show Mac status. Home Assistant can be connected alongside the Mac and remains the main connector on the other supported panels.
