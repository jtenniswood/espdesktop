# Screen settings

Choose **Customize Display** from the EspDesktop menu bar icon, or open the display’s local address in your browser. Select the **Screen** tab to change how the touchscreen looks and behaves.

Choose **Apply Configuration** at the bottom of the tab when you’re ready to restart the display and apply your changes.

## Appearance

- **Active Colour** sets the accent colour.
- **App Subpages** controls whether keyboard shortcut pages open when an app comes into focus and what happens when you switch apps. See [app page behaviour](/features/subpages#app-page-behavior).
- **Theme** offers Dark, Light or Automatic. Automatic follows sunrise and sunset or times you set.
- **Clock Bar** shows or hides the status bar on the home screen.

## Display

- **Backlight** controls brightness. Choose **Manual** for a fixed level, **Automatic** to follow sunrise and sunset, or **Timed** to set your own dawn and dusk times. Automatic and Timed let you set separate day and night levels.
- **Rotation** turns the interface by 0°, 90°, 180° or 270° to match how the display is mounted.

## Sleep & Schedule

- **Screensaver** can dim the screen, show a clock or turn the display off. Choose **Timer** to start it after idle time, or **App Connection** to start it when your paired computer locks or disconnects.
- **Night Schedule** sets a daily period to dim the screen, turn it off or show a clock. It takes priority over the screensaver. It is off on new displays. If you enable it with the preset hours and brightness, the screen dims to **15%** from **7:00 PM to 7:00 AM** in the display’s local time.

For the **Clock** screensaver, turn on **Show date** to include the local date or adjust the separate day and night clock brightness. When the screensaver dims the display, Automatic and Timed backlight modes also offer separate day and night dim levels.

When the night schedule uses **Screen Off**, set **When Woken, Idle Time to Screen Off** and **When Woken, Screen Brightness** to control how long and how brightly the screen wakes after a tap. The defaults are one minute and 10%.

When the display is asleep, tap once to wake it, then tap a card to use it.

### Computer screen lock {#mac-screen-lock}

When EspDesktop is connected, locking your computer also locks the touchscreen controls. The display shows **Locked** while its backlight is on. Unlock your computer to use the display again.
