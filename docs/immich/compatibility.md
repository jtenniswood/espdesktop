# Immich Photos

EspDesktop no longer consumes Home Assistant image entities, camera snapshots or photo metadata. The former Immich photo screensaver and related card setup are retired. Saved card fields remain readable in backups, but do not start photo downloads or slideshows.

For Home Assistant photo-frame integration, use the [EspControl documentation](https://jtenniswood.github.io/espcontrol/).

EspDesktop fullscreen artwork now comes from the paired [Mac Companion](/features/media-cover-art).
