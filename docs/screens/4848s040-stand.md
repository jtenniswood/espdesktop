# 3D-printable desk stand for the Guition 4848S040 {#_3d-printable-stand-for-the-guition-4848s040}

This optional case doubles as a desk stand, angling the 4-inch touchscreen so you can see and tap it next to your keyboard. Check that your display is marked **4848S040** before printing. [See the supported display](/screens/4848s040).

## Print the case

Use the print profile and settings for your printer on the model listing. [Get the print file on MakerWorld](https://makerworld.com/en/models/3592833-espdesktop-4inch-screen-stand#profileId-4115394).

## Order a print locally

If you don’t have a 3D printer, a local printing service can print the stand and deliver it to you. As a rough guide, one stand could cost **US$10–15**.

1. Download the 3MF file from [MakerWorld](https://makerworld.com/en/models/3592833-espdesktop-4inch-screen-stand#profileId-4115394).
2. Choose a service below and upload the file to its quote form or attach it to your quote request.
3. Ask for **one stand in black PLA** at the original size. Check the delivered price, including any minimum order charge, shipping and taxes.

## Fit the display

1. Unplug the USB-C cable and **remove the display’s original back plate**. This gives the printed case’s mounting clips the clearance they need.

   <a href="/images/4848s040-stand-back-plate-removed.jpg"><picture><source type="image/avif" srcset="/images/4848s040-stand-back-plate-removed-480.avif 480w, /images/4848s040-stand-back-plate-removed-960.avif 960w" sizes="(min-width: 768px) 688px, 90vw"><img src="/images/4848s040-stand-back-plate-removed.jpg" alt="Back of the Guition 4848S040 with the original back plate removed" width="1000" height="1000" loading="lazy"></picture></a>

2. Align the display with the printed case and gently press it into place until the clips engage.
3. Reconnect the USB-C cable and check that the plug seats fully and the cable has room.
