## Install the display {#set-up-espdesktop}

Open this guide in **Chrome** or **Edge** on your Mac to use the browser installer.

### Flash the firmware {#flash-the-firmware}

1. Connect your Guition 4848S040 to your Mac with a USB-C data cable.
2. Click **Install EspDesktop**, select the USB port that appeared when you connected the display, and follow the installer prompts. Keep the cable connected until the display restarts.

<!--@include: ../generated/screens/4848s040-install.md-->

### Connect the display to Wi-Fi {#connect-the-display-to-wifi}

1. On your Mac, join the temporary Wi-Fi network named on the display. If the setup page doesn’t open automatically, open the address shown on the display.
2. Select your **2.4 GHz** home Wi-Fi network, enter its password, and click **Save**.
3. When the display connects and prompts you to download the Mac app, reconnect your Mac to your home network.

[Get help connecting to Wi-Fi](/getting-started/troubleshooting#wifi-setup-and-reset-recovery).

## Download and pair the Mac app {#install-and-pair-the-espdesktop-mac-app}

Use an **Apple silicon Mac with macOS 13 or later**.

### Install the Mac app {#install-a-release}

Use the Mac app from the same release as your display firmware. For older firmware, [find the matching DMG under Assets on GitHub](https://github.com/jtenniswood/espdesktop/releases).

### Pair your display {#pair-the-display}

When you first open EspDesktop on your Mac, follow the on-screen instructions to pair it with your display.

To add another display, open **Mac Settings → Displays → Add Display** and enter the code from that display’s setup page. Both displays can stay connected and control the same Mac. The Displays page and menu bar show each display’s connection status and open its own **Customize Display** page. Choosing **Disconnect** pauses that display until you connect it again.
