# Set up EspDesktop on the Guition 4848S040 {#supported-display-4-inch-guition-4848s040}

Pair the 4-inch Guition ESP32-S3 4848S040 with your Mac to open apps and folders or run shortcuts from your desk.

</section>

<section class="display-setup-step" aria-labelledby="where-to-buy">

<div class="display-setup-copy">

## Buy the screen {#where-to-buy}

Choose the **4-inch Guition ESP32-S3 4848S040**. It has a square 480 × 480 screen. On the unit pictured in the [board identification photo](/images/4848s040-stand-back-plate-removed.jpg), the circuit board is marked **ESP32-4848S040** beside the Guition logo. Use the model number and listing photos to identify the display before buying.

For the first install, use the USB-C port on the display housing. The photo shows it beside the microSD slot. Check the seller's package contents and delivery estimate. Allow for a USB-C data cable and an optional [printable desk stand](/screens/4848s040-stand) when pricing the setup.

**Before buying**, check that you have an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 13 or newer. Intel Macs are unsupported. You also need a USB-C data cable, Chrome or Edge for flashing, and a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network that the display can share with the Mac.

## Install firmware {#install}

Connect your screen with a **USB-C data cable**. Open the [firmware installer and step-by-step guide](/getting-started/install) in Chrome or Edge.

<p id="connect-the-display-to-wifi" class="display-setup-anchor">After installing the firmware, follow the on-screen instructions to connect your display to Wi-Fi.</p>

## Set up your screen {#set-up-your-screen}

Once your screen is paired, click **Customize** in the Mac Companion app to open the display editor. Follow the [setup wizard](/features/first-run) to choose which apps appear on your screen.

After setup, customise your [Home screen](/features/home) and [subpages](/features/subpages) with folders, website links, shortcuts, and more.
