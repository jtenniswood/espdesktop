# App setup wizard

The setup wizard helps you build your first Home screen. Open the web editor for an empty display. If your Mac isn’t connected yet, the wizard starts with **Connect your Mac**, where you’ll find the Mac app download and pairing code. Once connected, choose **Next**. [Install and pair the Mac app](/getting-started/mac-app#pair-the-display).

## 1. Choose your apps

Use **Search apps** to find the Mac apps you use most often. Turn on the apps you want on your display, then choose **Next**. You can pick up to eight, depending on your display’s grid size. Setup leaves at least one tile free.

[![Choose your apps screen with a search field and a switch beside each app](/images/first-run-choose-apps.png)](/images/first-run-choose-apps.png)

## 2. Choose whether to add shortcut pages

The wizard selects **Add shortcut pages** when at least one of your chosen apps has built-in shortcuts. It adds the ready-to-use shortcuts automatically when you save, and you can edit them later. [Edit app shortcut pages](/mac-controls/apps#ready-made-app-subpages).

Choose **Launch apps only** if you want each card to open its app or bring it to the front. Select **Done** to save.

[![Shortcut pages screen showing Add shortcut pages and Launch apps only](/images/first-run-shortcut-pages.png)](/images/first-run-shortcut-pages.png)

## 3. Choose when shortcut pages open

If you added shortcut pages, choose how to open them.

- **Follow the active app** opens its shortcut page on the display when you switch to that app on your Mac.
- **Open from the display** lets you open the shortcuts by tapping the app’s card when that app is already active on your Mac.

Choose **Save setup**. You can change this choice later under **Screen → Appearance → App Subpages → Open app subpages on focus**.

[![Finish setup screen showing Follow the active app and Open from the display](/images/first-run-finish-setup.png)](/images/first-run-finish-setup.png)

## Your Home screen

Your app cards appear on the display and in the web editor’s **Layout** tab. A small arrow on a card shows that it has a shortcut page. Use the empty tile to add another control. [Add a Mac control](/features/setup#add-a-mac-control).

[![Example Home screen in the Layout tab with eight app cards and one empty tile](/images/first-run-home.png)](/images/first-run-home.png)

You can choose **Skip setup** to build your layout by hand. To run the wizard again, open **Screen → Appearance → App Subpages → Setup Again**. Saving again replaces your existing layout. Close the wizard with **×** to keep your current layout.
