# Set up your first touchscreen layout {#app-setup-wizard}

The setup wizard helps you build your first Home screen. Open the web editor for an empty display. If your computer isn’t connected yet, the wizard starts with **Connect your computer**. Follow the pairing steps for [macOS](/screens/4848s040#setup-on-macos) or [Windows](/windows-preview#pair-your-display), then choose **Next** once connected.

## 1. Choose your apps

Use **Search apps** to find the apps you use most often. Turn on the apps you want on your display, then choose **Next**. You can pick up to eight, depending on your display’s grid size. Setup leaves at least one tile free.

## 2. Choose whether to add keyboard shortcut pages

The wizard selects **Add keyboard shortcut pages** when at least one of your chosen apps has built-in keyboard shortcuts. It adds the ready-to-use keyboard shortcuts automatically when you save, and you can edit them later. [Edit app keyboard shortcut pages](/mac-controls/apps#ready-made-app-subpages).

Choose **Launch apps only** if you want each card to open its app or bring it to the front. Select **Done** to save.

## 3. Choose when keyboard shortcut pages open

If you added keyboard shortcut pages, choose how to open them.

- **When the app gains focus** opens its subpage when you switch to the app on your computer.
- **On app card press (default)** opens the app and its subpage with one press on the display.
- **On a second app card press** opens or switches to the app first. Once it’s active, press its card again to open the subpage.

Choose **Save setup**. You can change this choice later under **Screen → Appearance → App Subpages**.

## Your Home screen

Your app cards appear on the display and in the web editor’s **Layout** tab. A small arrow on a card shows that it has a keyboard shortcut page. Use the empty tile to add another control. [Add a card](/features/home#add-a-card).

<a href="/images/first-run-home.png"><picture><source type="image/avif" srcset="/images/first-run-home-480.avif 480w, /images/first-run-home-960.avif 960w" sizes="(min-width: 768px) 688px, 90vw"><img src="/images/first-run-home.png" alt="Home layout after setup with a Safari card and empty tiles for more controls" width="1554" height="1828" loading="lazy"></picture></a>

You can choose **Skip setup** to build your layout by hand. To run the wizard again, open **Screen → Appearance → App Subpages → Setup Again**. Saving again replaces your existing layout. Close the wizard with **×** to keep your current layout.
