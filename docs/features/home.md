# Customise the EspDesktop touchscreen layout {#home}

Home is the main screen on your EspDesktop touchscreen. Open your display’s address in a browser and select **Layout** to arrange the cards you want within easy reach.

<span id="move-and-resize-cards"></span>

## Organise cards

- **Move a card.** Drag it to another spot in the grid. If you drop it onto another card, that card shifts to the next available space.

- **Resize a card.** Hover over it and drag the handle in its bottom-right corner to make it wider, taller or smaller. The available sizes depend on the card and the space in the grid. Press **Escape** during the drag to keep the original size.

Moves and resizes save when you drop the card or release the handle. Choose **Save** when editing a card.

To select several cards, drag a box from the blank area beside, above, or below the preview, or from a gap between cards. On Home or a subpage, cards touched by the box are highlighted as you drag. Hold **Shift** to add to the selection, **Command** (**Ctrl** on Windows) to toggle cards, or press **Escape** to cancel the drag. Release the mouse, then right-click a selected card for actions such as **Copy**, **Cut** and **Delete**. Press **Backspace** or **Delete** to remove the selected cards from the layout.

To move or copy a card between Home and a subpage, right-click it and choose **Cut** or **Copy**, then right-click an empty tile on the destination page and choose **Paste**. Clipboard actions save the layout as you make them.

To copy a card to another display, right-click it and choose **Copy Code**. In the other display’s **Layout** editor, right-click an empty tile and choose **Paste Code**. Paste the code into the dialog, then choose **Paste** and check that the card fits and opens the intended app, folder, or action.

## Change how a card looks

Right-click a card to open its menu.

- **Name.** Choose **Edit Card**, change the **Label**, and select **Save**. You can also click a visible card name in the grid, type the new name and press **Enter**.
- **Background colour.** Open **Card Colour** and pick a preset or use the eyedropper to choose a custom colour. Choose **Reset Colour** to restore the default background.

## Add a card

Select **+** in an empty spot. See [Adding a card](/features/adding-a-card) for choosing a card type and setting its name, icon, and colours.

## Create a custom subpage

See the [Subpages guide](/features/subpages) to create a subpage and group related cards.

### Use and customise the Back card

To customise a Back card, edit its name and colours in the editor. Its default and active colours are saved with the subpage and included in backups.

Subpage and Back buttons change page as soon as you touch them. In the default app subpage mode, a single press opens the subpage while the Mac opens or switches to the app. You can also open subpages when an app gains focus or after pressing the card again. [App subpage options](/features/subpages#app-page-behavior).

Returning with **Back** or a swipe shows the destination page before loading any missing app artwork. Cached icons appear straight away; other icons can appear shortly afterward.
