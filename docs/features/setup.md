# 3. Configure your cards

## Open the display’s web editor

On your Mac, click the EspDesktop icon in the menu bar and choose **Customize Display**. You can also open the local address shown on the touchscreen in your browser.

If the setup wizard opens, choose your favourite apps and follow the prompts, or choose **Skip setup** to add cards yourself. The [app setup wizard guide](/features/first-run) walks through its shortcut options.

## Add a card {#add-a-mac-control}

1. Select **Layout** and click **+** in an empty tile.
2. Choose one of these card types. If the picker shows connector tabs, look under **Companion**.

   | Card | What to enter or choose |
   | --- | --- |
   | [Applications](/mac-controls/apps) | Pick the Mac app you want to open. |
   | [Key Shortcut](/mac-controls/shortcuts) | Choose a preset or enter your own key combination. Give EspDesktop **Accessibility** access on your Mac so it can send the keys. |
   | [Folder](/mac-controls/folders) | In **Mac Settings → Folders**, click **Add Folder…** and approve a folder. Then select it in the card editor. |
   | [Website](/mac-controls/websites) | Enter the web address you want to open. |

3. Click **Save**, then tap the new card on your display to try it.
