# Screensaver

Configure **Screensaver** in the web **Settings** tab.

- **Disabled** leaves the normal screen visible unless a night schedule applies.
- **Timer** starts the screensaver after the configured period without interaction.
- **App Connection** follows the paired Mac's connection and lock state.

Use **Then** to choose a dimmed screen, a clock, or display off. Brightness settings control how bright the dimmed screen or clock should be.

Touch the display to wake it. A wake during scheduled night hours uses the [Night Schedule](/features/screen-schedule) wake settings before returning to the overnight state.

[Idle](/features/idle) controls returning from a subpage to the home screen; it is separate from sleeping the display.

In **App Connection** mode, the panel stays awake while the Mac is connected and unlocked. Disconnection or locking immediately requests the selected sleep action without waiting for the idle timer. An abrupt power or network loss can take around 20 seconds to detect.
