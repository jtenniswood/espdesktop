The home screen starts with a **3-row x 3-column** grid. In **Screen > Display > Grid size**, choose a **4-row x 4-column** grid for **16 card slots**. The choice is saved across restarts. Cards outside the smaller grid are kept when you switch back to 3 × 3. The setting requires firmware with grid size support.

[Subpage](/features/subpages) folders have the same grid size, with one space reserved for Back (up to 15 cards in 4 × 4).

Flexible card sizes are supported: Single, Tall, Wide, and Large.
