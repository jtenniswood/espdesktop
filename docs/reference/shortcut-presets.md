# EspDesktop Mac keyboard shortcut presets

These are the presets defined in the EspDesktop keyboard shortcut catalog. The options on your display depend on your EspDesktop version and the app you select. Enable [macOS Accessibility access](/mac-controls/shortcuts#allow-accessibility-access) and bring the app to the front before trying a shortcut. Some shortcuts also need a particular window, view or selection.
