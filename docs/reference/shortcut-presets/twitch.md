# Twitch player shortcuts on an EspDesktop touchscreen

EspDesktop pairs a small touchscreen with your Mac. Its Twitch presets control the player in the Twitch Safari web app. Use them to pause, mute, switch to full screen, or create a clip.

## How do I set up Twitch controls?

Install Twitch as a Safari web app, then add it as an **Applications** card in EspDesktop.

1. Open **www.twitch.tv** in Safari and choose **File → Add to Dock**. Launch the installed Twitch web app.
2. [Pair your EspDesktop display](/features/first-run), [allow macOS Accessibility access](/mac-controls/shortcuts#allow-accessibility-access), and approve Twitch in the EspDesktop Mac app.
3. In the display’s web editor, open **Layout** and add an **Applications** card for **Twitch**. Enable **Add app subpage** for a page of player controls, or choose **Key Shortcut → Application → Twitch** for a single action.

## Why does a player shortcut do nothing?

Click the video player to focus it, then try **Mute / Unmute** or **Play / Pause** on the display. Seeking requires a recording or another player view with a seek bar. Clip shortcuts depend on the channel’s clip settings.
