# Twitch player shortcuts on an EspDesktop touchscreen

Put Twitch playback controls within reach on a small touchscreen beside your Mac. Tap to mute a stream when someone comes over, play it again when you’re ready, or go full screen without hunting for the player controls.

## What can I do with it?

Start with **Mute / Unmute**, **Play / Pause**, and **Toggle Full Screen**. Add **Clip Live Stream** if you like saving highlights on channels that allow clips.

## How do I set up Twitch controls?

1. Open **twitch.tv** in Safari and choose **File → Add to Dock**. Open Twitch from the Dock.
2. In the display editor, open **Layout** and add an **Applications** card for **Twitch**. Turn on **Add app subpage**, choose your controls, and save.

New to EspDesktop? [Set up your display](/features/first-run) and [enable keyboard shortcuts on your Mac](/mac-controls/shortcuts#allow-accessibility-access). If you manage which apps EspDesktop can open, approve Twitch in **Apps**.

## Why does a player shortcut do nothing?

Click the video in the Twitch app, then try **Mute / Unmute** on the display. To jump forward or back, use a recording or a player with a seek bar. To create a clip, choose a channel that allows clips.
