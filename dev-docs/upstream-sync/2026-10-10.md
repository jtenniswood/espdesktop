## Validation limits

Host, browser, configuration and documentation checks are recorded in each port PR. Full firmware builds run serially with ESPHome 2026.9.1. The JSON records build revisions. Builds produced by the final validation runner also record selected compiled-component hashes, checked against the authored files. Earlier builds and failed attempts without `compiledInputHashes` provide compiler-log evidence only, not compiled-input hash verification. A successful older build does not prove a later firmware revision. Historical failures remain visible after their fixes. Completed compiler logs are preserved in `2026-10-10-firmware-logs/`; each JSON record links its archive and records hashes for the original and compressed bytes. Superseded records describe queued jobs that did not run, rather than failed builds of that revision. No physical device testing or flashing has been performed. Keep all feature PRs open until device testing is confirmed. Browser firmware installation remains without authentication as explicitly chosen by the owner.
